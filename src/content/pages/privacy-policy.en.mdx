## Scope

This policy explains how Campaign Evolved Manual handles information when you browse this independent editorial guide. The public site does not currently offer user accounts, paid subscriptions, checkout, comments or a contact form.

## Information processed

The site may process limited technical information needed to operate, secure and improve the publication:

- **Server and security logs** may contain an IP address, request time, requested URL, response status, user agent and diagnostic data.
- **Aggregate analytics** may record page views, approximate region, device category, referrer and interactions so we can understand which guides are useful.
- **Local preferences** may be stored in your browser to remember choices such as the light or dark theme.
- **YouTube requests** are made only after you choose to play an embedded video. Until then, the page uses a locally hosted preview image and does not load the YouTube player.

We do not ask for account, profile or payment information on the public site.

## How information is used

Technical and aggregate information is used to deliver pages, prevent abuse, investigate errors, measure performance and prioritize editorial updates. It is not used to make decisions about individual visitors.

## Service providers and external sites

The site uses infrastructure and analytics providers, including Cloudflare and Google Analytics, which may process technical information under their own privacy terms. If you play an embedded YouTube video or follow an external link, the destination service receives the request and applies its own privacy policy.

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the site, its visitors or others.

## Retention and security

Operational and analytics data is retained only as long as reasonably needed for security, measurement and legal obligations, subject to provider settings. We use HTTPS, access controls and managed infrastructure, but no Internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices

You can block or delete browser storage, use browser privacy controls, block analytics scripts and avoid loading embedded videos. These choices may affect preference persistence or media playback but do not prevent access to the written guides.

Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you rights concerning personal information. Because the public site has no account system, we may need enough request details to identify the relevant technical record without collecting unnecessary information.

## Children

Campaign Evolved Manual is a general-audience gaming reference and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may revise this policy when the site, its providers or legal requirements change. The “last updated” date identifies the current version.
