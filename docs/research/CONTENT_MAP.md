# Content Map

## Editorial position

Campaign Evolved Manual is an independent, bilingual companion for mission preparation, systems reference and release information. It does not claim official status and does not replace the game.

## Verified release facts used on the site

- Release: July 28, 2026
- Premium early access: July 23, 2026
- 13 campaign missions, including three Operation: Meteor prequel missions
- Up to four-player online co-op
- Two-player local split-screen on supported consoles
- Cross-play and cross-progression
- Nine additional weapons available in the expanded sandbox

These facts were cross-checked against the supplied Steam and Halo Waypoint references on July 19, 2026. Claims not supported by those references are written as editorial guidance rather than official specifications.

## Mission model

Each mission article includes:

- Spoiler-light overview
- Recommended approach
- Objective sequence
- Encounter warnings
- Collectible/skull checklist
- Co-op notes
- Related arsenal/enemy/vehicle entries

## Language strategy

- English is the base locale at `/`.
- Simplified Chinese uses `/zh`.
- Route slugs remain locale-neutral so hreflang pairs are stable.
- All interface and editorial copy is sourced from Paraglide message functions.
